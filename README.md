# Harrison's World

Calm, simple tablet games about planets, fruit and veg, cars, the sea, and music, made for one little boy.

Open it at https://cawconsultancy-dev.github.io/harrisons-world/

## What it is

- 25 short games with big pictures, one clear spoken word at a time, and lots of praise.
- No timers, no scores, no losing. A "wrong" tap just wiggles and the right picture glows.
- Calm instrumental music throughout, with a music button on every screen.
- A grown-ups area (press and hold the person button) for sound, choices, and which games show.

## Put it on a tablet

- iPad: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
- Open it once with internet so it saves for offline use.
- Works best on iPadOS 15 or later, or an up-to-date Android tablet with Chrome.

## Privacy

No accounts, adverts, cookies, analytics, tracking, microphone, or links to other sites. Settings are saved only on the tablet using it. GitHub Pages, as the host, keeps visitor IP addresses in its own security logs.
